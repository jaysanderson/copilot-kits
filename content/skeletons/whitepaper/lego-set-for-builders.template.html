Trust you can measure

Every answer, scored

A demo that sounds confident is easy. A demo that proves it is right is the one that survives the room. Agentic RAG scores its own answers with REMi, a purpose-built evaluation model following the RAG Triad.

Answer relevance
0–5

How well the answer actually addresses the question that was asked.

Context relevance
0–5

How relevant the retrieved sources were to that question in the first place.

Groundedness
0–5

How firmly the answer is grounded in those sources, rather than invented.

Read the three together and you can diagnose a pipeline, not just grade it: strong context but weak groundedness points at the model; weak context points at retrieval. It runs live, per query, in the dashboard or through the API - and every answer is grounded and cited by default, so each fact traces back to its source.
