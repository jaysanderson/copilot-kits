**BUILD-TIME NOTE - RESOLVED (GM, 26 Aug 2026).** Two exchanges to get this right. The

# BUILD NOTES

**Slide count:** 32, which sounds like a lot for 15 minutes and is not - most are
statement slides held for ten to twenty seconds. Average is 28 seconds. Deck-builder may
merge adjacent statement slides where a build animation serves better, but must not add
words to compensate.

**The three callbacks are load-bearing.** The refrain at slides 5, 29 and 30. The 90/10
bar at slides 8, 10 and 27. These are what make it memorisable for him and quotable for
the room. Do not let a redesign break them.

**Every statistic on-slide carries its source and year** in small type. Sources are in
`research-synthesis.md`.

**The do-not-claim list in `research-synthesis.md` is binding.** In particular: nothing
about citizen developers coming true, no personalisation ROI figures, no claim that
marketing spend is rising, and Klarna is only ever "the wrong metric", never "AI failed".

**Voice check before this is final:** cold open with no throat-clearing (slide 1 holds),
hard-varied sentence length with deliberate fragments throughout, one honest complication
conceded and turned (movement 4), plain words, concrete numbers over adjectives, builds
to one reframe and restates it tighter (slides 5 to 30), collegial rather than
triumphant. Australian spelling, spaced hyphens, no banned words.
