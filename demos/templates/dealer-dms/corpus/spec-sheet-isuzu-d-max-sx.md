# Marketing Spec Sheet - Isuzu D-Max SX (2023)

**Body type:** Ute
**Example stock at Coppermill Motor Group:** CM-5145 (Riverside site)

## Overview
The 2023 Isuzu D-Max SX is a workhorse ute with genuine off-road capability and one of the more comfortable ride qualities in the segment unladen.

## Typical buyer profile
This model consistently attracts buyers prioritising running cost certainty
and dealer network support over outright performance, and is a reliable
recommendation for a customer whose brief is "something dependable that
won't surprise me."
