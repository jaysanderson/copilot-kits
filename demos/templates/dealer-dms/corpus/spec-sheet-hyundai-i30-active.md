# Marketing Spec Sheet - Hyundai i30 Active (2021)

**Body type:** Hatch
**Example stock at Coppermill Motor Group:** CM-5137 (Northgate site)

## Overview
The 2021 Hyundai i30 Active is a practical small hatch with class-competitive boot space and Hyundai's long factory warranty as a strong buyer reassurance point.

## Typical buyer profile
This model consistently attracts buyers prioritising running cost certainty
and dealer network support over outright performance, and is a reliable
recommendation for a customer whose brief is "something dependable that
won't surprise me."
