# Marketing Spec Sheet - Subaru Outback Sport (2021)

**Body type:** Wagon
**Example stock at Coppermill Motor Group:** CM-5171 (Northgate site)

## Overview
The 2021 Subaru Outback Sport is Subaru's rugged wagon with standard all-wheel drive and genuine light off-road ability most wagon buyers never fully use but appreciate having.

## Typical buyer profile
This model consistently attracts buyers prioritising running cost certainty
and dealer network support over outright performance, and is a reliable
recommendation for a customer whose brief is "something dependable that
won't surprise me."
