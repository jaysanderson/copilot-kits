## Finding
Stock CM-5234 (2022 Mitsubishi Triton GLS) intake recon found rear tyre tread at 2mm on both rear positions with visible uneven wear across the tread width, worse on the outer edge - consistent with a suspension alignment issue rather than simple wear-out. Four-wheel alignment check found the rear toe out of spec. Recommend correcting the alignment before fitting new heavy-duty TR-1180 band tyres, not simply replacing the tyres on the existing misalignment.
