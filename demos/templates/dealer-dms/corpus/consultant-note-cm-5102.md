## Finding
Intake inspection on Stock CM-5102 (2023 Toyota RAV4 GXL) found the front left brake pad at 2.6mm and the front right at 2.8mm, both below the 3mm replacement threshold in the Brake Pad and Rotor Service Standard. Rotors show light scoring but measure within the manufacturer's minimum thickness. Recommend BR-2210 pad replacement, front axle only, rear pads measured at 6.1mm and 6.3mm, within tolerance. No other mechanical findings. Paint grade 8/10, minor door edge chip on the driver's door, within normal detail scope.
