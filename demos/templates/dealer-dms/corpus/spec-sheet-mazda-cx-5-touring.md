# Marketing Spec Sheet - Mazda CX-5 Touring (2023)

## Overview
The 2023 Mazda CX-5 Touring is Mazda's refined mid-size SUV, with a noticeably upmarket cabin feel for the price point and confident highway manners.

## Typical buyer profile
This model consistently attracts buyers prioritising running cost certainty
and dealer network support over outright performance, and is a reliable
recommendation for a customer whose brief is "something dependable that
won't surprise me."
