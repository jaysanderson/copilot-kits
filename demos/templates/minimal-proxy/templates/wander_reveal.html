How this works

This page does not redraw the site's menu. It reads a knowledge graph of {{ f.pages }} University pages that links pages the tree of the site keeps apart, and every step you take is a real query against it.

  1. Your browser asks this app for the connections of one page. It never holds the knowledge base address or key.
  2. This app's own server sends one graph query to Progress Agentic RAG for that page, then one more for each shared thread it finds, so you see the other pages on the thread.
  3. The graph was written once, by rules rather than by a model, so building it cost nothing beyond the writes. Two kinds of link are shown here: {{ f.tree_links }} follow a page's place in the URL tree (the up and down strip), and {{ f.thread_links }} join a page to one of {{ f.threads }} threads we defined: {{ f.topics }} topics, {{ f.audiences }} audiences and {{ f.campuses }} campuses. A page joins a thread when its text mentions it at least three times, or the thread is in its title or address.
  4. Why a link exists is on every card. Usually it is a sentence from the page's own text that mentions the thread. For {{ f.title_only }} of the {{ f.thread_links }} links the only evidence is the title or address, and the card says "In the page title". Those are weaker, and they are marked as such.
  5. Step counts are tree distance: how many steps up and down the site's own URL tree separate two pages. They are not click counts; the real menu and logo offer shortcuts we have not measured.
  6. Ask only these pages sends your question to Progress Agentic RAG limited to the pages on this walk, with the walk passed along as context. The answer cites those pages.

Reach. Counted from this graph's own data when the server starts, over the {{ f.pages }} pages in it. From a typical page, the site's tree (parent, children and siblings) reaches {{ s.one_step.tree_median }} pages in one step and {{ s.two_steps.tree_median }} in two, which is {{ s.two_steps.tree_share }} per cent of the site. Shared threads reach {{ s.one_step.thread_median }} in one step and {{ s.two_steps.thread_median }} in two, which is {{ s.two_steps.thread_share }} per cent. "Tree" here is the URL tree, not the real menu, which has shortcuts we have not measured. {{ s.stranded }} pages have one or no neighbour in the tree and three or more through threads: pages the navigation strands.

The hole. {{ s.no_thread_pages }} of the {{ s.pages }} pages share no thread at all, because the threads are the {{ f.threads }} we defined. Your own taxonomy would replace ours, and that is the real fix.

Measured, not assumed. We asked five cross-cutting questions three ways: a plain ask, the same ask with the walk passed as context, and the same ask limited to the pages the walk reached. Limiting to the walk rescued one question the plain ask could not answer (an international student's English, scholarship and visa costs), and made no difference or a narrower answer on the rest; passing the walk as context alone changed little. So the graph is here to help you find and move between pages, not to out-answer a whole-site question.

What it cannot do. A page about something outside the threads shows fewer links. Two very busy pages (the art collection) return only their first 500 relations. The quoted sentences come from the text we crawled, which can include rotating banners, so a sentence may not be on the page at the moment you open it. We also wrote links between pages that share a rare proper name ({{ f.hidden_names }} of them); they are kept in the graph but not shown, because a shared name is not always a shared subject.
