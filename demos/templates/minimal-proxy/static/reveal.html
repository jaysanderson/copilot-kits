How this works

Every answer on this page comes from a real request to Progress Agentic RAG, run over a knowledge base of 343 pages crawled from university.edu.au. Nothing is pre-written.

1. Your browserYou ask in English, Chinese, Arabic or Bahasa Indonesia on the home page. Asking opens a results page that carries your question and language in its address. The question is sent as typed. It is not translated first.
↓
2. This app's own serverHolds the credentials, so no key ever reaches your browser. It pins a low-cost generative model and sends one request.
↓
3. Progress Agentic RAG: retrieval and generationA multilingual semantic index matches your question, in any of the four languages, to the English pages. A cited answer is generated in your language, and each claim is tied to the passages that support it.
↓
4. Back on the serverCitation positions become numbered markers at the claims they support, each resolved to the real university.edu.au page. If nothing sourced is found, the whole response is withheld rather than guessed.
↓
5. Your answerThe answer streams onto the results page as it is generated, with numbered citations at the claim, a source list, follow-up questions in the same conversation, and a next step that links out to University's own application, course or scholarship pages. Arabic answers display right to left.

Put it on your own site

The same ask, language choice and cited answer ship as an embeddable widget: one script tag and one mount point. It talks only to this server, so no key or knowledge base address is ever in your page.

<script src="https://your-app.fly.dev/static/embed/widget.js" async></script>
<div data-ask-widget data-langs="en,zh,ar,id"></div>

Optional data-scope limits answers to a section of the site (study, scholarships, how-to-apply, international). Unknown scopes are rejected rather than answered unscoped. For this demonstration the widget accepts requests from any website on purpose, so it can mount anywhere; a production deployment restricts it to your own domains.
