Brightwater Cafe Pty Ltd lodged this commercial claim after an electrical fire broke out in the cafe kitchen at Fortitude Valley, QLD on 11 April 2026 under policy TCP-71004. The fire started in the kitchen's own wiring, damaging the kitchen fit-out, equipment and part of the dining area, and forced the cafe to close for repairs. The claimant estimates the property damage and the loss of trade together at about $128,000 and seeks assessment of both the property loss and the business-interruption loss.
