This is the policy schedule for TideCommercial Property policy TCP-71004 held by Brightwater Cafe Pty Ltd for its premises at Fortitude Valley, QLD. Building/fit-out sum insured $210,000; contents, plant and equipment sum insured $140,000. Business-interruption cover: selected, with a sub-limit of $50,000. Standard excess $2,500. Cover selected: fire and smoke, storm and rainwater, impact, theft. Fire, including an electrical fire in the premises' own wiring, is a covered peril. The business-interruption loss is payable up to the $50,000 sub-limit, separately from the property loss.
