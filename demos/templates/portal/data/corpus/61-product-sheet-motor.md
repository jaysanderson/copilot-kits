TideDrive Motor is Publisher Mutual's comprehensive private motor vehicle product, covering accidental loss or damage to the insured vehicle, liability for damage to other property, and a standard suite of benefits including a hire car after an at-fault accident, new-car replacement in the first two years for a total loss, and a choice of agreed or market value at inception. The Glass Excess Waiver endorsement, when selected, removes the standard excess on a glass-only claim (windscreen, window or sunroof) and is a strong retention and cross-sell add-on brokers should offer at every quote.

Performance and aftermarket vehicle modifications - engine remaps, aftermarket exhausts, forced-induction changes, suspension and body modifications beyond manufacturer specification - must be declared at application and are individually underwritten; modified vehicles are not eligible for the standard online rate and must be referred. Drivers under 25 or within their first 12 months of an unrestricted licence attract a young/inexperienced-driver loading, which is materially higher again when combined with a modified vehicle. Target market is standard, unmodified passenger and light commercial vehicles for private use; rideshare, food-delivery and vehicles used for hire or reward are out of appetite for this product and should be referred to Publisher Mutual's commercial motor product instead.
