From 1 August 2026, Publisher Mutual is making a new optional cyber-incident endorsement available on TideCommercial Property. The endorsement covers business-interruption loss following a covered cyber incident (ransomware, business email compromise leading to fraudulent payment, or a confirmed data breach requiring notification under the Privacy Act), up to a sub-limit of $250,000, and includes access to an incident-response panel for affected policyholders. The endorsement is priced separately from the base Commercial Property rate and is not included by default.

Brokers must disclose the availability of this endorsement to every TideCommercial Property client at their next renewal falling on or after 1 August 2026, and record whether the client was offered and whether they selected it, consistent with the same disclosure-and-record standard that applies to optional flood cover on the Home and Contents product. This is a product update, not a mandatory cover change - a client may decline the endorsement - but the offer and the client's decision must be documented in the file. Clients operating in retail, professional services or any business holding customer payment-card or health data should be proactively flagged by the broker as a strong candidate for this cover given their elevated cyber-incident exposure.
