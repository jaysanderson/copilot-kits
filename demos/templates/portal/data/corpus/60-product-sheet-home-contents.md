TideProtect Home and Contents is Publisher Mutual's building and contents product for owner-occupied and landlord residential property. Standard cover is on an accidental-damage basis for both building and contents, including cover for fire, storm, escape of liquid, impact, theft and malicious damage, subject to the policy wording's exclusions. Flood cover - defined as the inundation of normally dry land by water escaping or being released from a river, creek, lake, dam or other natural watercourse - is an optional cover that must be actively selected on the schedule; it is not included by default, and this is the single most common source of a declined claim on this product, so brokers must confirm the client's flood exposure and actively offer the optional cover at every new-business and renewal placement.

Standard sub-limits include a $2,500 cap on any single unspecified valuable item (jewellery, watches, art) unless individually specified on the schedule, and a $10,000 cap on portable electronic equipment away from the home unless specified. The Glass Excess Waiver and Emergency Accommodation extensions are available as optional add-ons. Target market is owner-occupied and landlord residential dwellings of standard construction; the product is not designed for properties used for short-term letting platforms above 90 nights a year, heritage-listed dwellings, or properties in a mapped high bushfire-attack-level zone without confirmation of construction-standard compliance - refer these to underwriting rather than quoting on the standard rate card.
