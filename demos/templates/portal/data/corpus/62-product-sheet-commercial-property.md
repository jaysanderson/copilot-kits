TideCommercial Property covers small-to-medium commercial premises for building, stock and contents damage, with an optional business-interruption extension available up to a 24-month indemnity period. Cover is written on a declared-value basis; the client's declared sums insured for building and stock drive both the premium and the maximum payable, so brokers should remind clients to review declared values at every renewal, particularly stock sums insured, which are the most common source of under-insurance at claim time.

Warehousing and distribution risks storing flammable or hazardous retail stock - aerosols, solvents, cleaning chemicals, paints - require a completed fire-risk supplementary questionnaire at application and are referred to underwriting rather than quoted on the standard rate card, particularly where the premises lacks an automatic fire sprinkler system or dedicated segregated storage for that stock. A prior fire, flood or business-interruption claim in the last five years is also a standard referral trigger. Target market is retail, light-industrial, warehousing and professional-office premises of standard construction with adequate fire protection for their stock profile; heavy manufacturing, food processing with open-flame cooking, and premises with a prior uninsured or disputed claim are out of appetite.
