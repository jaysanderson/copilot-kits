Assessment by Owen Fitzgerald (Property Claims Adjuster). The cause of loss is confirmed as riverine flood: the water entered the Delacroix home from the river overtopping its banks and from overland flow, consistent with the catchment rainfall on 28 May 2026 and the water line and silt deposits observed on site. This is flood as defined in the policy wording, not storm or rainwater run-off and not an escape of water from within the home. The policy schedule for THP-55190 shows optional flood cover was NOT selected. On that basis the flood damage is not covered. The claim is held at needs-more-information pending confirmation of the water source with the claimant and the local flood authority, but on the current evidence the flood loss is not payable under this policy.
