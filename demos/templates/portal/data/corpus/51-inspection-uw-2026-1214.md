An independent property inspector attended 14 Ridgeline Close, Katoomba, NSW on 18 July 2026 to assess the risk for underwriting application UW-2026-1214. The inspector confirms the dwelling is a single-storey brick-veneer construction with a Colorbond steel roof, built in accordance with BAL-29 bushfire construction requirements, and notes that the compliance certificate sighted on-site matches the certifier reference declared in the application. The rear boundary is confirmed at approximately 12 metres from continuous bushland canopy, with a mown, low-fuel buffer maintained between the fence line and the tree line at the time of inspection.

The inspector confirms ember-guard mesh has not yet been fitted to the subfloor and roof vents, consistent with the application's disclosure, and recommends this be treated as an outstanding underwriting condition rather than a completed mitigation. No sprinkler or ember-defence system is present. Guttering is fitted with leaf guards, and the property has a static water tank of approximately 5,000 litres with a petrol-driven pump connection suitable for ember-attack firefighting, which the inspector notes as a positive mitigating factor not mentioned in the original application. Access to the property is via a single sealed road with no secondary egress, which the inspector flags as a factor relevant to bushfire risk in a rapid-onset event. Overall the inspector assesses the property as a heightened bushfire-exposure risk consistent with its BAL-29 rating and its position, pending completion of the ember-guard mesh installation.
