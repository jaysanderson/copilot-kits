Assessment by Owen Fitzgerald (Property Claims Adjuster), with a building inspector's report attached. The inspector attributes the water damage behind the bathroom wall and under the floor to gradual deterioration of the shower waterproofing membrane over an extended period, likely many months, rather than a single sudden event. Gradual deterioration, gradual leakage and lack of maintenance are excluded under the TideProtect Home & Contents wording, which covers only sudden and accidental water damage. On the current evidence the loss appears to be gradual and therefore excluded. The claim is held at needs-more-information pending any further evidence from the claimant that the damage was sudden and accidental rather than gradual.
