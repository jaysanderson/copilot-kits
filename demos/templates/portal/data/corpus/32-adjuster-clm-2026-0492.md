Assessment by Aisha Okonkwo (Claims Adjuster). The burglary is confirmed by forced entry to the rear door and the police report. The contents loss is genuine. However, the diamond ring claimed at about $6,000 was not listed as a specified item on schedule THP-62255, so under the policy's contents rules it is subject to the $2,500 unspecified single-item sub-limit; the most payable for the ring is $2,500. The remaining stolen contents are payable to their sums insured. This is an over-sub-limit exception, not a decline: the claim is partially payable, with the ring capped at the sub-limit and the balance of contents paid, less the $400 excess.
