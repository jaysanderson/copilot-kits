A vehicle inspector examined the 2023 Corvale GTS Turbo hatchback at the applicant's home address in Springvale, VIC on 4 July 2026 as part of underwriting application UW-2026-1229. The inspector confirms the aftermarket performance exhaust and ECU remap declared in the application are present and match the compliance paperwork supplied by the fitting workshop, which is a licensed automotive workshop registered in Victoria. Engine output with the remap is confirmed at approximately 18 percent above the manufacturer's original specification. No further undeclared modifications were identified. The vehicle's odometer reading, tyre condition and general presentation are consistent with a well-maintained vehicle of its age and declared use.

The inspector notes that performance modifications of this kind are associated with a higher claims-frequency profile in Publisher Mutual's own motor book, particularly for drivers under 25 on a provisional licence, and recommends this be reflected in the underwriting decision alongside the applicant's licence status and prior claim. The inspector found no basis to dispute the declared agreed value of $38,000 given the vehicle's condition and modification history.
