Publisher Mutual's underwriting risk-data feed confirms the applicant's disclosed prior fire claim of approximately $85,000 two years ago, with no other prior claims recorded for the entity across the six years of trading history available to the feed. No adverse credit or insolvency flags are recorded against Southbank Import Traders Pty Ltd or its directors. The applicant has held continuous commercial property cover since incorporation, with the prior claim being its only loss on record.

The risk-data feed's industry banding for warehousing and distribution of packaged consumer goods that include aerosol or other flammable retail stock shows a materially higher average fire-severity outcome than general dry-goods warehousing, consistent with the inspector's finding on unsegregated aerosol storage. Properties in this industry band without an automatic sprinkler system and without segregated flammable-stock storage fall into the feed's highest fire-severity tier for the Commercial Property product line. The feed notes that a prior fire loss combined with an unresolved fire-severity risk factor - here, the absence of sprinklers and stock segregation - is a standard trigger for referral to a senior underwriter rather than automatic acceptance at standard terms.
