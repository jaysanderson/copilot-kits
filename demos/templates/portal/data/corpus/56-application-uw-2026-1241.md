Southbank Import Traders Pty Ltd has applied for a TideCommercial Property policy covering its single warehouse facility at 22 Freight Drive, Yatala, QLD, used to store and distribute imported packaged consumer goods including household aerosol products. The applicant declares a building sum insured of $2.4 million and stock sum insured of $1.1 million at any one time, with business interruption cover requested for an indemnity period of 12 months. The warehouse is a tilt-panel concrete construction of approximately 3,200 square metres, built in 2014, with a single roller-door vehicle entrance and one designated pedestrian fire exit.

The application discloses one prior claim: an electrical fault in a first-floor distribution board caused a contained fire in a storage bay two years ago, resulting in a paid claim of approximately $85,000 for stock and building damage under the applicant's previous insurer. The applicant states the distribution board and affected wiring were fully replaced and certified following that incident. The application does not mention whether an automatic fire sprinkler system is installed; this is noted as a field requiring inspection confirmation.
