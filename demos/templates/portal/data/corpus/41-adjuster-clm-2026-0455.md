Assessment by Marcus Trent (Senior Claims Adjuster), with the fire brigade report and a loss adjuster's report attached. The fire is confirmed as an electrical fire originating in the cafe kitchen's own wiring on 11 April 2026 - a covered peril under the TideCommercial Property wording. The property damage to the fit-out, equipment and dining area is within the sums insured on schedule TCP-71004. The business-interruption loss from the forced closure is payable up to the $50,000 sub-limit selected on the schedule. Total assessed loss about $128,000, comprising the property loss within the property sums insured and the business-interruption loss up to its sub-limit. Recommendation: payable, less the $2,500 excess.
