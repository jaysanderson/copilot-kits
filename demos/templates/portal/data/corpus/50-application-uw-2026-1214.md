Nadia and Simon Cheng have applied for a new TideProtect Home & Contents policy on their newly completed home at 14 Ridgeline Close, Katoomba, NSW. The dwelling is a single-storey brick-veneer home completed in March 2026, replacement value declared at $780,000, with contents declared at $95,000. The property backs directly onto a declared bushland reserve, with the rear boundary fence sitting approximately 12 metres from the tree line. The applicants have held no prior home insurance claims and have been continuously insured with their previous insurer for six years at their former address in Springwood, NSW, with a clean claims history.

The application discloses that the property sits within a mapped bushfire-prone area under the relevant NSW planning provisions, and that the home was constructed to a Bushfire Attack Level (BAL) rating of BAL-29 under the current bushfire construction standard. The applicants note that ember-guard mesh has been ordered for the subfloor and roof vents but has not yet been fitted, with installation expected within eight weeks of the application date. There is no on-site sprinkler or ember-defence system. The nearest fire hydrant is approximately 800 metres from the property by road, and the local rural fire brigade station is 6 kilometres away. The applicants request cover to commence within 14 days of assessment.
