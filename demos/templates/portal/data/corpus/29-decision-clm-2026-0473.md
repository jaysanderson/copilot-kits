Coverage decision on claim CLM-2026-0473 for Helena Vaughn, policy THP-88213. Publisher Mutual has assessed the water damage at the Newtown property and determined the claim is PAYABLE. The cause of loss - a sudden and accidental burst of a flexible hose under the kitchen sink - is a covered peril under the TideProtect Home & Contents wording, and no exclusion applies. Settlement will be by repair and replacement of the damaged flooring, cabinetry and contents, assessed at about $18,400, less the $400 standard excess.
