Helena Vaughn reports sudden water damage at her home in Newtown, NSW under policy THP-88213. On 14 June 2026 a flexible hose connected to the mixer tap under the kitchen sink burst without warning while the family was out, releasing water across the kitchen and into the adjoining living area over several hours. The damage affects flooring, the lower kitchen cabinetry and some contents. The claimant describes it as a sudden burst, not a slow leak, and estimates the loss at about $18,400.
