Publisher Mutual issues the TideCommercial Property policy to cover a small business's premises, stock, plant and equipment, and, where selected, its business income. This Product Disclosure Statement sets out the covered perils, the sums insured, and the conditions that apply. Cover is provided only for the sums insured and options shown on the policyholder's own policy schedule; where this wording and the schedule differ, the schedule prevails.

Covered perils. Subject to the exclusions and conditions below, TideCommercial Property covers loss or damage to the insured property caused by: fire and smoke, including an electrical fire originating in the premises' own wiring or equipment; storm, rainwater and wind; impact, including impact by a vehicle; and theft involving forcible and violent entry. An electrical fire that starts in the business's own kitchen or plant is a covered peril where it is sudden and accidental.

Sums insured and co-insurance. The building, contents, stock and plant are each covered up to the sum insured shown on the schedule. Cover is subject to a co-insurance (average) condition: if the sum insured is materially less than the full replacement value of the property at the time of loss, any claim payment may be reduced in the same proportion. Policyholders should keep sums insured current with replacement values.

Business interruption and its sub-limit. Where business-interruption cover is selected and shown on the schedule, Publisher Mutual covers the loss of business income and the additional costs of continuing to trade following an insured event that damages the insured premises, up to the business-interruption sub-limit shown on the schedule. The property damage and the business-interruption loss are assessed separately: the property loss is payable up to the property sums insured, and the business-interruption loss is payable up to its own sub-limit. A single fire claim can therefore be payable in two parts, each capped by its own limit.

Excess. The excess shown on the schedule is deducted from the amount otherwise payable for each claim before payment.
