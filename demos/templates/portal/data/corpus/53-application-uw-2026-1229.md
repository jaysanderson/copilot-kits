Jack Petrovic, aged 19 and holding a P2 provisional licence for eight months, has applied for a TideDrive Motor comprehensive policy on a 2023 Corvale GTS Turbo hatchback, declared agreed value $38,000. The application discloses that the vehicle has an aftermarket performance exhaust and an ECU remap increasing engine output above the manufacturer's original specification, both fitted by a licensed workshop with modification compliance paperwork available. The applicant discloses one prior at-fault motor claim eleven months ago, a rear-end collision at low speed with an estimated repair cost of $4,200, settled under his previous insurer's comprehensive policy.

The applicant's declared use is private, with an estimated annual driving distance of 18,000 kilometres, and the vehicle is garaged overnight at a home address in Springvale, VIC. No other drivers are declared on the policy. The applicant requests the lowest available excess option and asks whether the modifications affect the quoted premium.
