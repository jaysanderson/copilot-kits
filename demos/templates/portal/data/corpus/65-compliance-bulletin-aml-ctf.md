This bulletin sets out Publisher Mutual's anti-money-laundering and counter-terrorism-financing (AML/CTF) requirements for brokers placing TideCommercial Property business. Standard customer due diligence - verified business name, ABN, and beneficial-ownership declaration for any entity with 25 percent or greater ownership - is required on every commercial placement regardless of premium size. Enhanced due diligence applies where any of the following are present: a combined sum insured above $5 million, a premium-funding arrangement with a third-party lender, a newly incorporated entity with less than 12 months trading history, or a beneficial owner who is a politically exposed person.

Where enhanced due diligence applies, the broker must obtain and retain source-of-funds documentation for the premium payment and confirm the beneficial-ownership chain to natural persons before binding cover, and must not bind cover on a provisional basis pending this documentation. Brokers identifying any transaction pattern inconsistent with the client's stated business activity, or a client reluctant to provide beneficial-ownership information, must escalate to Publisher Mutual's compliance team before proceeding rather than declining the placement unilaterally, since an unusual pattern may itself be reportable. This requirement applies in addition to, not instead of, each broker's own AML/CTF program obligations under the relevant Commonwealth legislation.
