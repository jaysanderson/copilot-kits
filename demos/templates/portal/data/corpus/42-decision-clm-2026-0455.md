Coverage decision on claim CLM-2026-0455 for Brightwater Cafe Pty Ltd, policy TCP-71004. Publisher Mutual has assessed the fire loss and determined the claim is PAYABLE. The electrical fire is a covered peril under the TideCommercial Property wording. The property damage is payable within the building and contents sums insured, and the business-interruption loss is payable up to the $50,000 sub-limit selected on the schedule. The total settlement is about $128,000, less the $2,500 excess, and the claim is proceeding to settlement.
