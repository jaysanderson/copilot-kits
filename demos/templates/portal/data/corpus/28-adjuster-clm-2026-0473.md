Assessment by Aisha Okonkwo (Claims Adjuster). The cause of loss is a burst flexible hose under the kitchen sink, confirmed by the plumber's report and the pattern of water damage radiating from the sink cabinet. This is a sudden and accidental escape of water, which is a covered peril under the TideProtect Home & Contents wording and is included on schedule THP-88213. There is no evidence of a gradual leak or lack of maintenance. The loss is assessed at about $18,400. Recommendation: payable, subject to the $400 standard excess.
