Publisher Mutual issues the TideDrive Motor policy as comprehensive motor cover for the vehicle shown on the policy schedule. This Product Disclosure Statement sets out the covered events, the excesses, and the conditions that apply. Cover is provided only in accordance with the policyholder's own policy schedule; where this wording and the schedule differ, the schedule prevails.

Covered events. Subject to the exclusions and conditions below, TideDrive Motor covers accidental loss or damage to the insured vehicle caused by: collision or impact, including a single-vehicle collision; fire; theft of the vehicle, and attempted theft; storm, hail and flood affecting the vehicle; and malicious damage. Windscreen and window glass breakage is covered as a specific benefit and is described below.

Excess. An excess is the amount the policyholder contributes to each claim. The standard excess shown on the schedule is deducted from the amount otherwise payable for each claim. An additional age excess applies where the driver at the time of the incident is under 25 years of age. A windscreen or glass-only claim attracts the glass excess shown on the schedule, unless the optional Glass Excess Waiver endorsement is held (see below).

Windscreen and glass, and the Glass Excess Waiver endorsement. Publisher Mutual covers the repair or replacement of the insured vehicle's windscreen and window glass where it is broken by an insured event. A glass-only claim normally attracts the glass excess shown on the schedule. Where the policyholder holds the optional Glass Excess Waiver endorsement, recorded on the schedule, no excess applies to a windscreen or glass-only claim: the claim is payable in full with a nil excess. The Glass Excess Waiver applies only to glass-only claims and does not change the excess on any other type of claim.

Theft conditions. A theft claim requires that the vehicle was secured and that reasonable care was taken to protect it. Publisher Mutual may require a police report or event reference number for a theft claim, and will assess the date of loss against the supporting evidence.

Duty of disclosure. Before entering into or renewing the policy, the policyholder has a duty to take reasonable care not to make a misrepresentation to Publisher Mutual, and to disclose everything they know, or could reasonably be expected to know, that is relevant to the insurer's decision to insure and on what terms. If the policyholder does not meet this duty, Publisher Mutual may reduce or refuse a claim, or cancel the policy.

Premium and cover while in arrears. Cover under this policy depends on the premium being paid as required. Where the premium is unpaid and the policy is in arrears at the date of loss, Publisher Mutual may reduce or refuse the claim to the extent permitted by law, or treat the policy as not in force for that loss. A claim handler must check the premium payment status as at the date of loss before a theft or total-loss claim is settled.
