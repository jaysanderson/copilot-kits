A commercial fire-risk inspector attended 22 Freight Drive, Yatala, QLD on 25 June 2026 for underwriting application UW-2026-1241. The inspector confirms the warehouse has no automatic fire sprinkler system installed; fire protection is limited to portable extinguishers at intervals through the racking area and a manual fire alarm panel connected to a monitored service. The inspector confirms the replaced distribution board and rewiring from the prior fire claim, sighting the electrical compliance certificate dated eighteen months ago, and finds no evidence of further electrical deficiencies at the time of inspection.

The stock storage includes packaged household aerosol products held in original manufacturer cartons on standard pallet racking, mixed among other packaged goods rather than segregated in a dedicated flammable-goods area. The inspector notes this as a materially elevated fire-severity factor: an ignition in this racking bay would have access to aerosol stock capable of significantly accelerating a fire, and recommends segregated, bunded storage for the aerosol stock as a risk-improvement condition. The single pedestrian fire exit is confirmed clear and compliant with current signage, but the inspector notes it as the sole means of pedestrian egress besides the vehicle roller door, which is not to be relied upon as a fire exit. Overall the inspector rates the property as an elevated fire-severity risk pending the fire-suppression and stock-segregation recommendations above.
