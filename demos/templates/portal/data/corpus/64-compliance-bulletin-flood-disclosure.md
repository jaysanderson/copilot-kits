---
title: Compliance Bulletin - Flood Cover Disclosure Obligations
slug: compliance-bulletin-flood-disclosure
summary: Broker obligation to actively disclose and offer optional flood cover on every Home and Contents placement, and to record the client's decision.
doc_type: Compliance Bulletin
published: 2026-05-04
---

This bulletin reminds all brokers distributing TideProtect Home and Contents of their obligation, under the current General Insurance Code of Practice and Publisher Mutual's distribution agreement, to actively disclose that flood is an optional cover on this product and is not included by default. At every new-business quote and every renewal, the broker must inform the client in plain language that flood cover is optional, provide the client's property-specific flood-model rating where available, and record the client's decision to include or exclude flood cover in the file notes. A quote or renewal that does not include this disclosure and record is a compliance breach regardless of whether the client ultimately selects flood cover.

This obligation exists because flood-coverage misunderstanding is the leading cause of disputed claims and complaints to the external dispute resolution scheme on this product line. Brokers should not rely on the client having flood cover under a previous policy or with a previous insurer as evidence of informed consent for the current placement; the disclosure and record must be made fresh at every placement with Publisher Mutual. Any placement identified in a compliance audit without this record will be referred back to the broker for remediation and may affect the broker's authority to bind cover without prior referral.
