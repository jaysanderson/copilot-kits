The Delacroix Family lodged this claim after their single-storey home at Maribyrnong, VIC was inundated on 28 May 2026 when the nearby river overtopped its banks following sustained heavy rain across the catchment. Muddy water entered the home to a depth of around 400mm, damaging flooring, plasterboard, kitchen cabinetry and contents on the ground floor. The claimant describes the water as having come up from the river and the street, not from a burst pipe inside the home. Estimated loss is about $42,000. The claimant asks that the claim be assessed under policy THP-55190.
