Publisher Mutual issues the TideProtect Home & Contents policy to cover a policyholder's home building and its contents against a defined set of insured events. This Product Disclosure Statement sets out what is covered, what is excluded, and the conditions that apply. Cover is provided only for the sums insured and options shown on the policyholder's own policy schedule; where this wording and the schedule differ, the schedule prevails.

Covered perils. Subject to the exclusions and conditions below, TideProtect Home & Contents covers loss or damage caused by: fire and smoke; storm, rainwater and wind (but not flood, which is a separate optional cover described below); sudden and accidental escape of water or liquid, including water escaping from a burst flexible hose, a failed washing-machine connection, a burst pipe or an overflowing fixed appliance; theft or burglary, and attempted theft, involving forcible and violent entry to the home; impact, including impact by a vehicle, a falling tree or a falling aerial; and accidental breakage of fixed glass. Sudden and accidental water damage is a covered peril: a one-off escape of water, such as a flexible hose under a kitchen sink that bursts without warning, is exactly the kind of event this cover responds to.

Flood is optional cover. Flood means the covering of normally dry land by water that has escaped or been released from the normal confines of any watercourse, lake, reservoir, canal or dam, including a river that overtops its banks. Flood is NOT included as standard. Flood cover applies only where the policyholder has selected the optional flood cover and it is shown as included on the policy schedule. Where flood cover has not been selected on the schedule, loss or damage caused by flood is not covered, even though storm and rainwater damage may be covered.

Exclusions. Publisher Mutual does not cover: loss or damage caused by gradual deterioration, gradual or long-term water leakage, seepage, rising damp, rust, corrosion, wear and tear, or a defect that develops over time; loss or damage caused by a lack of maintenance or by failing to repair a known fault; loss or damage where flood cover was required but was not selected on the schedule; and wilful or reckless acts by the policyholder. The distinction between sudden and accidental water damage (covered) and gradual water damage (excluded) is decided on the evidence of how the damage occurred: a sudden burst is covered, while damage that developed slowly over weeks or months from a slow leak or poor maintenance is excluded.

Contents sub-limits and specified items. Unless an item is listed as a specified item on the schedule, any single contents item is covered only up to the unspecified single-item sub-limit of $2,500. Valuables such as jewellery, watches and fine art that are worth more than the unspecified single-item sub-limit must be listed individually as specified items on the schedule, with their value, to be covered for their full amount. If a valuable item worth more than $2,500 is not listed as a specified item, the most Publisher Mutual will pay for that item is the $2,500 unspecified single-item sub-limit; the remaining loss is not covered.

Excess. An excess is the amount the policyholder contributes to each claim. The standard excess shown on the schedule is deducted from the amount otherwise payable for each claim, before payment. Some optional covers and endorsements vary the excess; where an endorsement waives or changes the excess, the schedule records it.
