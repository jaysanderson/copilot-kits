# Motif: body of knowledge (`m-bok`)

A loose pile of an organisation's content tiles is swept by two green agent bands,
snaps into an ordered, labelled grid, a green boundary draws around it, and faint grey
"open internet" tiles are pushed out. Performs: "reads YOUR content, not the open internet".

**When to use:** any beat claiming grounding on the customer's own corpus, ingestion/
organisation, or the bounded-corpus trust story.

**Variables** (declare on the host `<html>`, bound by `data-var-text`):
`bokLabel1..bokLabel5` (grid category chips), `bokTab` (boundary tab, default "Your
content"), `bokOutside` (default "Open internet").

**Timing:** internal timeline 11.2s; sweeps 0.6-2.4, snap 1.0-4.2, labels 2.0-4.6,
legibility raise 4.6-5.4, boundary 5.3-6.6, push-out 5.6-7.2, settled-but-alive to the end. Event constants sit at
the top of the script - re-derive from the narration alignment when reusing.
Host slot: `data-composition-src=".../body-of-knowledge.html"`, id `m-bok`.
Fonts: expects `ProgDisplay`/`ProgText` @font-face in the host (brand/fonts woff2).
