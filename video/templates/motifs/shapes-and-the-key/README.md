# Motif: shapes and the key (`m-sk`)

Two use-case shapes, performed. **`/docs`**: three document families feed the reader, a
cited answer writes itself with a thread back to each family, and the join between
documents and reader draws SOLID - the first solid join in the film - while the other two
products fade to grey outlines and sit unused. **`/rows`**: the reader extracts structured
rows from a document, the row slides toward a table to join it and STALLS, because the
table's rows each carry a key chip and the extracted row's key slot is an empty dashed
outline; a key is then written onto the SOURCE DOCUMENT, propagates into the extracted row,
and only then does the join complete in long-dashed amber.

**When to use:** any use-case or pattern beat. `/docs` is the cheapest-first-step shape;
`/rows` is the documents-to-rows shape.

## What each phase is actually arguing

**`/docs` is the simplest picture in its film on purpose.** The solid join reads as relief
because every join before it has been dashed or broken, so do not place a solid join
earlier in a host film without a reason.

**`/rows` stalls on an ABSENT KEY, not on the extraction.** That is the whole point: the
difficulty is not the pipeline, it is that the key exists in your tables but exists on the
document only if someone put it there. The fix happens on the document, at frame left, and
the completed join is amber because that is whose work it is. **Never make the stall look
like an extraction failure.**

**Variables:** `skFam0..skFam2` (document families, defaults Manuals / Release notes /
Tickets), `skAvail` (default "AVAILABLE TODAY"), **`skBatches`** (the SCOPE the marker depends on,
default "Loaded in batches" - set beneath the solid join, not in a footnote, because a
marker shown without its precondition claims more than its evidence), `skIsv` (default
"ISV BUILDS"), `skKey`
and `skKey2` (the key chip on the document and on the extracted row, default "Order
number" for both - keep them identical, it is the same key).

**Timing:** internal timeline 15.1s, phases at `/docs` 0.00-8.29 and `/rows` 8.29-15.01.
Event constants at the top of the script: `AT_SHAPES` 2.14, `AT_DOCS` 3.33, `AT_AVAIL`
5.42, `AT_NEITHER` 6.87, `AT_ROWS` 8.29, `AT_KEYDOC` 11.90, `AT_PUTIT` 13.84. These are
the `sa-use-cases` b6 word events rebased to motif zero. **Re-derive from the host's own
narration alignment when reusing** - the key must be written on "put it there".

Host slot id `m-sk`. Fonts: expects `ProgDisplay` / `ProgText` in the host.

**Gated by:** the `sa-use-cases` render (shots B6 and B6b). Both phases are rendered there.

**Overlap to be aware of:** the executive film builds its own documents-to-rows motif under
a local `dtr-` prefix, because that shape is its hero where it is this film's secondary
beat. The two are deliberately different gestures - this one's emphasis is the ABSENT KEY.
Flagged to the lead rather than resolved here.
