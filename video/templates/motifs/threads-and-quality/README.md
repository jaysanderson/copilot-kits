# Motif: threads back to sources + quality (`m-tq`)

The answer card and six source tiles (same geometry as `m-rn`'s end state). SIX green threads draw - one per source chip on screen, six for six - from answer sentences back to source tiles, each tile pulsing as its thread
lands; a quality badge appears and its ring fills, settling on a QUALITATIVE label.
Performs: "every answer shows its sources, and trust is measured".

**When to use:** citations / provenance beats and the REMi-style groundedness story.
NEVER put a numeral in the badge (B34 / Field Rule 5) - the ring deliberately stops
short of full.

**Variables:** `tqBadge` (default "Well supported"), `tqChip` (default "Grounded
response"), `tqSources` (default "SOURCES").

**Timing:** internal timeline 12.4s; threads 1.3-4.2 (6 x 0.42 stagger), badge in 3.95, ring fill 4.15-8.0,
label 7.9, settle push 8.7-12.3. Pair after `m-rn` for continuity (shared card/stack
coordinates: card 830,330 730x460; stack x388, y400+66i).
