# Motif: the joins are yours (`m-jy`)

Three phases over one layout. **Phase A** ("the joins"): the three product objects perform
their verbs at once, briefly, so "each does its own job" is ONE picture; then three joins
try to draw between their notches, each starting solid, breaking into long dashes partway,
and stopping short with a small recoil so it reads as failing rather than pausing. As they
break, the legend writes itself. **Phase B** (`/count`): the trio compresses into a 5 x 3
field of fifteen seam markers; one join draws solid, one goes short-dashed and ENLARGES rather
than hiding, thirteen take long-dashed amber in an 80ms wave, and the counts set beneath.
**Phase C** (`/authors`): one of the thirteen enlarges and its join is WRITTEN stroke by
stroke, then resolves into three numbered segments. Performs: "each product works, almost
nothing joins, and thirteen of fifteen are yours to build".

**When to use:** any beat stating the joins thesis or the fifteen-pattern count. Phase A
teaches grammar rule 2 and **must run before any other join appears in a film**.

## THREE STATES, FOUR LABELS - the distinction is load-bearing

The legend writes in **two groups separated by a rule**, and that is not decoration:

- Three **states of the architecture**, each a stroke on the join: **solid** AVAILABLE
  TODAY, **long dash** ISV BUILDS, **short dash** NOT BUILT.
- One further **label**, below the rule: **UNCONFIRMED**, a state of our KNOWLEDGE, drawn
  as a question mark set ON a line whose own stroke is unchanged. It is deliberately not a
  fourth stroke.

An architect reads "nobody has established this" differently from "this is not built", and
the rule in the legend is what stops a film collapsing them. Follows `primer.md:21` as
corrected 9 Oct 2026; an earlier wording described four states and is superseded. **Never
restyle UNCONFIRMED as a fourth stroke.**

## Indices worth knowing before you re-time

`AVAIL_IDX = 4`, `NOTB_IDX = 10`, `HERO_IDX = 7`. **`HERO_IDX` is one OF THE THIRTEEN by
construction**, so a host that enlarges it in `/authors` gets "the hero is one of the
thirteen" structurally, without a line of narration doing the work.

**Variables:** `jyS0..jyS3` (the four labels in the legend), `jyS0b..jyS2b` (the three
labels under the counts), `jyNote` (the line under UNCONFIRMED, default "Not a state of
the architecture"), `jyCap` (the field caption, default "15 applied patterns").

**No label slot is provided on the enlarged marker.** A host that needs one overlays it in
its own composition, positioned over the marker. A host should own its own captions, so
this motif does not grow a slot for them.

## Gate coverage, stated rather than assumed

**Phases A and B are gated by the `sa-use-cases` render** (shots B2 and B2b).

**Phase C `/authors` is NOT rendered by `sa-use-cases` and is therefore NOT covered by its
gate.** It was built for the executive film, which specified the join-authoring gesture,
and it is gated by that film's render. Anyone reading a green gate report on
`sa-use-cases` should not take it as evidence about `/authors`: 2 of 3 phases measured.
