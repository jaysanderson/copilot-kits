Progress Agentic RAG finds things and shows its working.

## The cross-check, and the defect that produced it

**Corrected 9 Oct 2026.** The B6 row of the table above listed two ghost panels, `On premises`
and `Air gapped`, where the narration names three. `STORYBOARD.md` had all three, so my own
two files disagreed about what the picture shows.

**Why it mattered more than a missing label.** That beat states a negative AS A PICTURE, so
the picture carries the claim. A viewer who reads the frame and not the subtitle would have
learned that two things are not claimed and inferred the third was. On a residency beat, in
front of this room, that is the inference we least want.

**How it was found, and how it was not.** The sibling found it by reading my narration against
my own shot table. Neither file is wrong on its own terms: the narration line is correct and
the storyboard shot is correct. **When one file states a claim and another carries the picture
of it, checking each file against itself cannot find the disagreement.** My own on-screen
string scan had even listed the two strings and I read that list without noticing what was
missing from it, because a presence check can only ever confirm what is there.

**The check now exists:** `check_crossfile.py`. Every check in it reads ACROSS files; none
reads one file alone. Check 4 is this defect generalised past this film: every item the voice
names in a list of negatives must be present as an on-screen string, and the list is parsed
out of the narration rather than hardcoded, so rewriting the line cannot silently narrow the
check. 45 checks, 0 failures, exit 0. `--selftest` plants a positive into each check's
population and all six fire, including a plant that reproduces this exact defect by deleting
`Offline` from the table again.
