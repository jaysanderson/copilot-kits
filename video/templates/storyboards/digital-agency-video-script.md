## Beat 4 - What makes it a foundation, not a feature

**Eyebrow:** Why it holds up under a technical client
**Headline:** Understood at ingest. Planned at query. Governed on every answer.
**On-screen flow (three nodes, matches seller-video s04):**
Refine -> Retrieve -> Reason -> **Grounded answer**
**Subcaption:** Every answer respects the permissions of whoever's asking.

**Narration:**
"What makes it a foundation and not just a feature is what happens to the knowledge.
At ingest, agents label it, map it into a knowledge graph, and screen it for safety, so
it arrives understood, not just stored. At query time, a retrieval agent plans the steps.
It refines the question, retrieves the evidence, and reasons over what it found before it
answers. And every answer respects the permissions of whoever is asking. That's the
detail your most technical client's IT lead will ask about, and the detail that lets you
answer with confidence."
