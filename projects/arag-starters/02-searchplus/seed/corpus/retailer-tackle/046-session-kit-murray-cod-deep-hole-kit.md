# Session Kit: Murray Cod Deep Hole Kit

For deep, murky holes and sunken-timber structure where Murray cod hold, worked slowly
and precisely rather than searched quickly.

## The kit

1. **Tidewalker Deep Vibe 3/4oz** - the primary lure, vertical-jigged directly over the
   deepest structure with a controlled lift-and-drop.
2. **Tidewalker Deep Diver 120** - a cast-and-wound or trolled option for working past
   snags and rock bars in slightly shallower, less murky stretches of the same system.
3. **Backwater Crayfish Craw 3"** - a slower, bottom-hugging soft plastic option worked
   with a lift-and-drop presentation through timber once the vibe has raised interest
   without a hook-up.

See the *Species Profile: Murray Cod* and *Retrieve Guide: Vertical Jigging Deep
Structure* for the reasoning and technique detail.
