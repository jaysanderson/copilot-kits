# Tidewalker Shadow Minnow 90

**Length:** 90mm | **Weight:** 12g | **Buoyancy:** true suspend
**Price:** $24.95

## Why anglers reach for it

A true-suspending jerkbait sits motionless in the water column on the pause instead of
rising or sinking away - exactly where a following predator gets the longest look and the
most time to commit. In murky or low-light water, that hang time matters more than the
lure's colour, since a bass or barra is often working off vibration and silhouette rather
than fine detail.

## Targets

- **Barramundi** - a subsurface follow-up when the topwater bite is quiet, worked over
  low-light or tannin-stained water.
- **Australian bass** - the standard murky-water bass presentation when a spinnerbait's
  profile is too big for pressured fish.

## Best water conditions

- **Murky / low-visibility water** - rattling internals and a wide wobble on the twitch
  give fish something to track without needing to see it clearly.
- **Low-light (dawn, dusk, overcast, tannin-stained water)** - the suspend action shows
  best when fish are actively feeding but light is poor.

## How to work it

Cast past the target, let it settle to its suspend depth, then work a **twitch-and-pause**
retrieve: two sharp rod twitches to make it dart and flash, then a genuine pause - two to
four seconds - where it hangs dead still. Most strikes come on the pause. Vary the pause
length until fish tell you what they want that day.
