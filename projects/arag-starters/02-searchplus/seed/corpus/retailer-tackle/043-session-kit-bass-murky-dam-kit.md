# Session Kit: Bass Murky Dam Kit

For the classic post-rain scenario - a dam or river gone murky and Australian bass keyed
onto vibration over sight.

## The kit

1. **Northline Spinnerbait Twin Willow 3/8oz** - the primary lure. Slow-roll past every
   piece of standing timber and rock bar you can reach.
2. **Northline Lipless Rattler 70** - the faster search option for covering open murky
   water between timber, cast-and-wound.
3. **Tidewalker Shadow Minnow 90** - a subsurface backup for fish following the
   spinnerbait without striking; twitch-and-pause through the same water.

See the *Species Profile: Australian Bass* and *Water Conditions Guide: Murky Water* for
the full reasoning, and *Lipless Cranks vs Spinnerbaits* for when to lean on which of the
first two.
