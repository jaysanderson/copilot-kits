# Retrieve Guide: Twitch-and-Pause

The twitch-and-pause retrieve is built around a suspending hardbody - most often the
**Tidewalker Shadow Minnow 90** - that hangs motionless in the water column instead of
rising or sinking when you stop winding. That pause is where the technique does its work.

## The rod action

Two sharp, short rod twitches - enough to make the lure dart and flash but not enough to
move it far - followed by a genuine pause. Reel in slack between twitches, not during the
pause; the lure needs to hang still, not drift on a tightening line.

## Pause length

Start with a two-second pause and extend it if fish are following without striking - in
cold water or heavily pressured spots, pauses of four seconds or more often out-fish a
faster cadence. Most strikes come during the pause, as a following fish finally commits to
a target that isn't moving away.

## Best applications

- **Barramundi** in low-light or tannin-stained water, subsurface when the topwater bite
  is quiet.
- **Australian bass** in murky water, as an alternative to a spinnerbait when fish are
  following a bigger profile without committing.
