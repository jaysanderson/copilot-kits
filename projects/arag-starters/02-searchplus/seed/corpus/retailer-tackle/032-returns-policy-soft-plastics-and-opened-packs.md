# Returns Policy: Soft Plastics and Opened Packs

Soft plastics across the Sable Series range cannot be returned once the resealable pack
has been opened, in line with standard tackle-industry hygiene practice - plastics that
have been handled or exposed to water can't be resold.

## Unopened packs

Unopened Sable Series packs can be returned within 30 days of purchase with proof of
purchase, provided the pack seal is intact and the product hasn't been exposed to heat
that's caused colour bleed or softening.

## Wrong item ordered

If the wrong colour or size was ordered and the pack is still sealed, exchange for the
correct item is available within the same 30-day window at no extra charge.
