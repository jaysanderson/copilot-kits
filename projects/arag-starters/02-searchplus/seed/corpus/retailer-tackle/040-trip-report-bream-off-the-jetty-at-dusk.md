# Trip Report: Bream Off the Jetty at Dusk

*Submitted by a Drift Line customer.*

Fished the local jetty from about an hour before dark, which is when this spot always
switches on. Started with the **Northline Bibbed Minnow 50**, casting tight against the
pylons and twitching it back with pauses right on the shadow line - had three follows in
the first twenty minutes but nothing committed.

Once it got properly dim I switched to a **Sable Series Curl Tail Grub 3"** on a
drop-shot rig and just shook it in place right against the same pylons. That's when they
turned on - five bream in the last half hour of light, all on the finesse rig once the
hardbody had clearly put them on alert. Worth carrying both next time rather than picking
one.
