# Trip Report: Flathead on the Flats

*Submitted by a Drift Line customer.*

Gin-clear water over the sand flats near the boat ramp, dead low tide just starting to
run back in. Rigged a **Sable Series Paddle Tail 4"** on the **1/4oz Sable Series Jighead
Round** exactly as the product page suggests for that depth and just walked the edge
casting up-current, letting it swim down and across.

Flathead don't hit like bream - it's a solid thump and then dead weight. Had four fish
over the session, biggest just under 70cm, all in water I could see the bottom of the
whole time. Didn't need to change weight all session since the tide stayed manageable, but
would go the 3/8oz if I fished it again once the run-in really picked up.
