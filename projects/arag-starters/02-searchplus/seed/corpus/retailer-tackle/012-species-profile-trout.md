# Species Profile: Trout

Rainbow and brown trout in Australia's cold, clear highland streams and lakes are
easily spooked and respond to small, natural-profile lures fished with more stealth than
speed. Approach and presentation size matter more than for almost any other species Drift
Line stocks for.

**Go-to lures:**
- **Northline Surface Cicada 40**, walked across still, glassy water at dawn when trout
  are up looking for terrestrial insects on the surface.
- **Northline Trolling Minnow 130**, trolled at a slow, steady pace through deeper lake
  water and along drop-offs.

## Water conditions

Clear water and still/flat dawn conditions are when trout feed most visibly and
predictably; low light reduces the stealth needed and is the best time to fish smaller
water where trout are easily spooked.
