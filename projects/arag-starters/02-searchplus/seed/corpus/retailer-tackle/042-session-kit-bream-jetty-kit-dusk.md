# Session Kit: Bream Jetty Kit - Dusk

Built for the exact scenario Drift Line staff get asked about most: bream holding around
jetty pylons and pontoons as light drops toward dusk. Three presentations, worked in
order as the bite shifts through the session.

## The kit

1. **Northline Bibbed Minnow 50** - start here while there's still light. Twitch-and-pause
   tight against the pylons and pontoons, pausing right on the shadow line.
2. **Sable Series Curl Tail Grub 3"** - rigged for a drop-shot finesse presentation, the
   follow-up once the hardbody has drawn follows without committed strikes. Shake it in
   place tight to the same structure.
3. **Tidewalker Prawn Profile 75** - a soft-vibe blade worked with a lift-and-drop
   presentation for bream that have moved off the immediate structure into deeper water
   around the jetty as full dark approaches.

## Why this order

Bream get progressively more cautious as a hardbody works a piece of structure, so
stepping down from a moving presentation to a stationary finesse rig as light fades
follows how the fish's mood actually changes across the session - see the *Bream* species
profile guide and *Water Conditions Guide: Heavy Cover and Structure* for the reasoning
behind each stage.
