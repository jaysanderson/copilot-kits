# Returns Policy: Incorrect Order

If an item was ordered in error - wrong size, colour or range - it can be returned
unused, unopened and in original packaging within 30 days for a full refund or exchange.

## Condition required

Hardbodies and blades must show no sign of having been rigged or cast; soft plastics must
have an intact, unopened seal (see *Returns Policy: Soft Plastics and Opened Packs* for
full detail on that range specifically).

## Exchange versus refund

Exchange for the correct item is processed faster than a refund and is available
in-store immediately where stock allows; a refund follows standard processing once the
returned item is received and checked.
