# Session Kit: Barra Dawn River Kit

For a first-light session on a tannin-stained river - still water, low light, barramundi
feeding hard on the surface disturbance and vibration that carry through stained water.

## The kit

1. **Backwater Popper 65** - start on top the moment there's enough light to see your
   lure land. Pop-pop-pause tight to the bank.
2. **Tidewalker Shadow Minnow 90** - switch subsurface if the topwater bite is quiet or
   once the sun gets high enough to push the surface bite off. Twitch-and-pause with long
   pauses.
3. **Backwater Walk-the-Dog Stickbait 100** - an alternative topwater profile for the same
   window if the popper's been worked hard without success; a different action can trigger
   a follow that's stalled on the popper.

See the *Species Profile: Barramundi* and *Water Conditions Guide: Low Light and
Tannin-Stained Water* for the reasoning behind the order and retrieve details.
