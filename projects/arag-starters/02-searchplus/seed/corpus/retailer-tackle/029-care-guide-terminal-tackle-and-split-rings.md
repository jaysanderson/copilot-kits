# Care Guide: Terminal Tackle and Split Rings

Regardless of which Drift Line range you're fishing, the terminal tackle - split rings,
swivels and hook points - is the part of the system that fails first and costs the fish,
not the lure body itself.

## Before every trip

Run a thumbnail across every hook point on the lures going in the tray; a point that
catches your nail is sharp, one that slides is not. This takes under a minute for a
typical session's worth of lures and prevents more lost fish than any other single habit.

## Split rings

Check for any ring that's started to open under light pressure - squeeze gently with
pliers to confirm it's still fully closed. A ring that's been opened once to swap a hook
and not fully closed again is a common, avoidable failure point.

## Swivels on rigged plastics

If you're running a swivel above a soft plastic rig, check it still turns freely - a
seized swivel transmits line twist straight into the retrieve and will eventually cause
a break at the knot.
