# Species Profile: Australian Bass

Australian bass (*Percalates novemaculeata*) are structure-oriented ambush feeders found
through freshwater impoundments, rivers and their tributaries down the east coast. They
hold tight to timber, rock bars and overhanging bank cover and feed hardest around dawn,
dusk and any time visibility drops.

## The murky-water bite

When rain lifts colour into a dam or river, or wind stirs the shallows, bass shift from
sight-feeding to tracking prey through vibration and flash on their lateral line. This is
the single most reliable trigger condition for bass, and it's why murky water - not clear
 -  is when Drift Line staff fish hardest for them.

**Go-to lures:**
- **Northline Spinnerbait Twin Willow 3/8oz**, slow-rolled past timber and rock bars - the
  standard murky-water bass presentation.
- **Northline Lipless Rattler 70**, cast-and-wind through more open, sparser cover.
- **Tidewalker Vibe Blade 1/2oz**, lift-and-drop through deeper murky holes when bass have
  pulled off the bank.

## Clear-water and low-light alternatives

In clearer water, particularly at dawn and dusk, bass will also eat a **Northline Surface
Cicada 40** worked with a walk-the-dog surface retrieve over calm water, or a **Tidewalker
Shadow Minnow 90** twitched past bank cover.

## Water conditions that produce bass

Murky/low-visibility water and heavy cover/structure are the two conditions to prioritise;
still, low-light water at dawn is the best secondary window when the water is clear.
