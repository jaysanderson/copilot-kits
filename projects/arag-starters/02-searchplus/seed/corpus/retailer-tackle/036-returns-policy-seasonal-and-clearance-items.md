# Returns Policy: Seasonal and Clearance Items

Items marked as clearance or end-of-season stock are sold as final unless a
manufacturing defect is found - the standard 30-day change-of-mind return that applies to
full-priced stock does not extend to clearance-tagged items.

## Manufacturing defects still apply

The standard *Warranty Policy* still covers a genuine manufacturing defect on a
clearance item; only the change-of-mind return window is excluded.

## Identifying clearance stock

Clearance items are tagged at the point of sale and noted on the order confirmation  - 
check your confirmation if you're unsure whether an item falls under this policy before
requesting a return.
