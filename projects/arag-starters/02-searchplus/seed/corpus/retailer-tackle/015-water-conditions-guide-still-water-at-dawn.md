# Water Conditions Guide: Still Water at Dawn

The dawn "glass-off" - still, flat water before any wind has picked up - is prime topwater
time. A surface lure's disturbance carries further and looks more deliberate without chop
to break it up, and most predatory species feed hardest in this first hour of light
regardless of species.

## What to tie on

- **Backwater Popper 65** - the standard still-water dawn choice for **barramundi**,
  chugged with pauses over glassy water tight to the bank.
- **Northline Surface Cicada 40** - a smaller surface profile for **Australian bass** and
  **trout** feeding on insects on a still lake or river surface.
- **Backwater Frog Hollow-Body 60** - a weedless option for bass and mangrove jack holding
  in still water under overhanging cover.

## Why still water works

Without chop to disturb the surface, every pop, walk and splash from a topwater lure reads
as an isolated, deliberate event to a fish below - which is exactly what draws a strike
from a fish that's already looking up. As soon as wind picks up and breaks the surface,
switch to a subsurface presentation from the *Low-Light* or *Murky Water* guides instead.
