# Water Conditions Guide: Murky Water

Rain run-off, wind-stirred shallows and turbid rivers all drop underwater visibility to a
point where fish stop hunting by sight and start hunting by vibration and pressure waves
picked up on their lateral line. The lure choice that follows is consistent across
species: bigger profile, more vibration, more noise.

## What to tie on

- **Northline Spinnerbait Twin Willow 3/8oz** - the standard murky-water choice for
  **Australian bass**. Twin willow blades throw maximum flash and thump; slow-roll it past
  any structure.
- **Northline Lipless Rattler 70** - a faster search option with a tighter, higher-pitch
  vibration and internal rattle, cast-and-wound through sparser murky-water cover.
  See *Lipless Cranks vs Spinnerbaits* for exactly when to pick one over the other.
- **Tidewalker Deep Vibe 3/4oz** - vertical-jigged for **Murray cod** and **golden perch**
  holding deep in murky water.
- **Tidewalker Shadow Minnow 90** - a suspending subsurface option for bass and barramundi
  when a slower, twitch-and-pause presentation is called for.

## The retrieve matters as much as the lure

In murky water, a **slow-roll retrieve** at a steady, unhurried pace usually out-fishes a
fast retrieve - fish need time to track the vibration before they can commit, and a lure
moving too fast outruns their ability to home in on it.
