# Water Conditions Guide: Clear Water

In clear water fish are hunting by sight, which flips the murky-water logic: a natural
profile, realistic colour and a slower, quieter presentation out-fish anything bulky or
flashy - and pressured fish in clear water are often actively avoiding anything that looks
unnatural.

## What to tie on

- **Sable Series Paddle Tail 4"** - the standard clear-water flathead soft plastic over
  sand, rigged on a **1/4oz Sable Series Jighead Round** for typical flats depth.
- **Northline Bibbed Minnow 50** - a natural-wobbling hardbody for bream around structure,
  twitched rather than burned through.
- **Sable Series Curl Tail Grub 3"** on a drop-shot finesse rig for bream and whiting that
  have already seen a hardbody.
- **Northline Trolling Minnow 130** - trolled at a natural pace for trout in clear lake
  water.

## Presentation

Slow down. A **cast-and-wind steady retrieve** at reduced speed, or a **drop-shot
finesse** presentation that lets the lure hang in place, both give clear-water fish the
long, unhurried look they need before committing - rushing the retrieve is the single
most common reason a clear-water session goes quiet.
