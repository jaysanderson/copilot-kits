# Care Guide: Topwater Lures

Topwater lures like the **Backwater Popper 65**, **Backwater Walk-the-Dog Stickbait 100**
and **Backwater Frog Hollow-Body 60** are fished tight to structure more than almost any
other lure type, which means more hook-point wear from clipping timber and pylons on the
retrieve.

## Popper cup care

Rinse the concave face of a popper thoroughly - algae and salt build-up in the cup
changes how much water it throws on the pop, dulling the exact sound and splash that
draws strikes.

## Hook points after structure fishing

Check hook points after every session fished tight to cover; a point that's clipped
timber even once can roll enough to cost a hook-set. File back to a clean point rather
than fishing on a dull one.

## Hollow-body frogs

Check the weedless hook is still riding correctly against the body after any session
through weed or lily pads - a hook that's shifted out of position either snags constantly
or fails to set on the strike. Reseat it by hand before the next trip if it's moved.
