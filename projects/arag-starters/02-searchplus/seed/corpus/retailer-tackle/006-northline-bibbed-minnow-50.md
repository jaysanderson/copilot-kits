# Northline Bibbed Minnow 50

**House range:** Northline
**Length:** 50mm | **Weight:** 4.2g | **Dive depth:** 0.5-1m
**Price:** $19.95

## Why anglers reach for it

A small bibbed minnow with a tight wobble and a shallow dive puts it right in the zone
bream hold in around jetties, pontoons and rock walls - close enough to structure to draw
a reaction bite without a profile so big it spooks pressured, clear-water fish.

## Targets

- **Bream (yellowfin bream)** - the primary application around structure in clear water.
- **Flathead (dusky flathead)** - worked over adjacent sand edges on the same retrieve.

## Best water conditions

- **Clear water** - bream in clear water are often sight-feeding and respond well to a
  natural wobble at close range.
- **Heavy cover and structure** - built to be cast tight to pylons, pontoons and rock
  walls and worked back past the shadow line where bream sit.

## How to work it

Cast tight against the structure and use a **twitch-and-pause** retrieve - two short
twitches to bring it off the shadow line, then a pause of a second or two right where a
bream would be sitting. Bream often follow before striking, so keep the pauses in tight to
the structure rather than working it straight back to the boat.
