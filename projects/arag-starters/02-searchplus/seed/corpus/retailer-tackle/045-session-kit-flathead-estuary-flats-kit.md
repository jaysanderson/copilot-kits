# Session Kit: Flathead Estuary Flats Kit

For a session working clear sand and mud flats for dusky flathead through a changing
tide.

## The kit

1. **Sable Series Paddle Tail 4"** - the primary soft plastic, cast up-current and
   retrieved slow and steady along the bottom.
2. **Sable Series Jighead Round - carry 1/8oz, 1/4oz and 3/8oz** - start on 1/4oz for
   typical 1-3m flats depth and step up or down as the tide's current strength changes
   through the session.
3. **Northline Bibbed Minnow 50** - a hardbody backup for working the sand edges adjacent
   to any structure on the flat, such as a rock bar or old jetty piling.

See the *Species Profile: Flathead* and *Rigging Guide: Jighead Weight for Soft Plastics*
for full detail on matching weight to depth and current.
