# Post-Trip Full Rig Checklist

A short, consistent routine after every saltwater trip is what actually keeps a season's
worth of Drift Line gear fishing properly, rather than any single product's build quality.

## The routine

1. **Rinse everything in fresh water** - rods, reels, lures still on leaders, and the tray
   itself, not just the lures individually.
2. **Hook points** - run a thumbnail check across every hook that fished cover or bottom
   this trip; file or swap out anything that's rolled.
3. **Split rings** - squeeze-check any that took a hard hit against structure.
4. **Dry before storing** - lay hardbodies and blades out to dry fully before returning
   them to a sealed tray; trapped moisture causes more rust than open-air storage ever
   does.
5. **Separate soft plastics** back into their original packs by colour before they go back
   in the tray, to avoid overnight colour bleed.

Five minutes at the end of a session is the difference between gear that's still fishing
correctly next trip and gear that quietly degrades all season.
