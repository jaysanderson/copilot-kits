# Rigging Guide: Jighead Weight for Soft Plastics

Getting jighead weight right is the single biggest lever on how well a soft plastic like
the **Sable Series Paddle Tail 4"** or **Sable Series Curl Tail Grub 3"** fishes - too
light and it never reaches the strike zone in current, too heavy and the retrieve speeds
up past what a flathead or bream will chase.

## Sable Series Jighead Round - the reference weights

- **1/8oz** - skinny water under 1m, or a session where you need the slowest possible
  drop and retrieve without dragging bottom hard.
- **1/4oz** - the standard estuary-flats pairing, 1-3m of water with light to moderate
  current. This is the weight most Drift Line flathead and bream sessions are rigged on.
- **3/8oz** - deeper water beyond 3m or stronger tidal current, so the plastic still gets
  down and holds position rather than swinging up and away in the flow.

## How to check you've got it right

Cast and count the drop to the bottom - it should take a slow, unhurried count, not an
instant plummet or an endless drift. If it's instant, size down; if it never seems to
land, size up. Re-check after the tide changes, since current strength through the same
spot can shift enough to need a different weight within the same session.
