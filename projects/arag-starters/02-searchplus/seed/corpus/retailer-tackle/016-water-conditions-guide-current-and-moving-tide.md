# Water Conditions Guide: Current and Moving Tide

Current concentrates bait and forces fish to hold in predictable ambush positions - current
seams, eddies behind structure, and the drop-offs a run-out tide exposes. Fishing current
well is more about positioning the cast than working the lure hard.

## What to tie on

- **Sable Series Paddle Tail 4"** - cast up-current and let it swim down and across on a
  slow retrieve for **flathead** holding on sand edges as the tide runs.
- **Northline Lipless Rattler 70** - casts well across current seams for **golden perch**
  holding on the edge of the flow.
- **Tidewalker Deep Diver 120** - trolled or cast-and-wound past rock bars for **Murray
  cod** and **golden perch** in river current.
- **Backwater Spoon Wobbler 28g** - cast across current and allowed to swing for **tailor**
  and **barramundi** feeding on baitfish pushed by the flow.

## Positioning the cast

Cast up-current of the structure or drop-off you're targeting and let the current do the
work of sweeping the lure past it naturally - a lure fighting directly against or with the
current looks unnatural and fish notice.
