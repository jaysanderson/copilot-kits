# Backwater Popper 65

**House range:** Backwater - Drift Line Tackle Co.'s topwater and heavy-cover finesse range.
**Length:** 65mm | **Weight:** 11g | **Cup:** deep-face chugger
**Price:** $18.95

## Why anglers reach for it

Barramundi feed hardest in the low light of first and last light, and a surface bait
worked over still water is one of the most reliable ways to draw a strike from a barra
that's already keyed onto movement on top - especially in tannin-stained rivers where a
subsurface lure loses visibility fast but a surface disturbance carries.

## Targets

- **Barramundi** - the headline application, worked over still, low-light water including
  tannin-stained rivers where visibility underwater is poor but surface commotion still
  draws fish.
- **Mangrove jack** - worked tight to mangrove-lined structure at dusk.

## Best water conditions

- **Still / flat water** - a popper's chug and splash carry best without chop to compete
  with, classic dawn glass-off conditions.
- **Low-light (dawn, dusk, overcast, tannin-stained water)** - barra sit shallow and
  aggressive at these times regardless of water clarity underneath.

## How to work it

Cast tight to the bank or structure and let the rings settle for a few seconds before the
first pop - barra often watch before committing. Use a sharp downward rod flick to make it
"chug" and throw a splash, pause, then repeat: two or three pops, a two-second pause, repeat.
Most first-light strikes come in the pause, not the pop itself, so resist the urge to work
it continuously.
