# Species Profile: Bream (Yellowfin Bream)

Yellowfin bream hold tight to structure - jetty pylons, pontoons, rock walls and moored
boat hulls - and in the clear water typical of most bream estuaries they are notoriously
line- and lure-shy, especially once a jetty has seen a few casts.

## Structure fishing in clear water

Bream sit in the shadow line a structure throws, ambushing anything that drifts or swims
past. A natural, small-profile presentation worked slowly and quietly outperforms
anything bulky.

**Go-to lures:**
- **Northline Bibbed Minnow 50**, twitched past pylons and pontoons with pauses right on
  the shadow line.
- **Sable Series Curl Tail Grub 3"** on a light jighead, worked with a drop-shot finesse
  presentation for fish that have already seen a hardbody go past.
- **Sable Series Micro Jig 1/16oz**, the finesse option when bream are sitting deep in the
  structure's shade and won't commit to anything moving fast.

## Low-light structure sessions

Bream around jetties feed hardest as light drops at dusk, when they move up onto the
shallower edges of structure to feed - see the *Bream Jetty Session Kit - Dusk* for the
full loadout for this exact scenario.

## Water conditions

Clear water and heavy cover/structure are bream's defining conditions; current at the
turn of the tide, moving bait past the structure, is the best secondary trigger.
