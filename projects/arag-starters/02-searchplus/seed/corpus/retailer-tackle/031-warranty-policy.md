# Warranty Policy

Drift Line Tackle Co. warrants hardbody lures, blades and vibes across the Tidewalker and
Northline ranges against manufacturing defects in the body, bib and factory-fitted split
rings for 12 months from the date of purchase.

## What's covered

Paint or clear-coat failure not caused by impact, factory-fitted split rings that fail
under normal use, and bib or body cracking not caused by a strike, snag or drop.

## What's not covered

Damage from striking structure, being snagged and pulled free, hook points (a wear item,
not a defect), and any lure that's been modified from its factory rigging.

## Making a claim

Bring the lure and your receipt or order confirmation to any Drift Line store, or contact
support with photos of the fault and your order confirmation. Claims are assessed against
the fault described above, not against general wear.
