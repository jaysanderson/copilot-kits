# Trip Report: Murray Cod from the Deep Timber

*Submitted by a Drift Line customer.*

Found a deep hole against a bank of sunken timber that's produced before, water the colour
of milky tea after the river came up. Dropped the **Tidewalker Deep Vibe 3/4oz** straight
down along the edge of the timber and worked it vertically - lift and controlled drop,
watching the line the whole time.

Fourth or fifth drop, felt the line jump on the fall rather than a hit on the lift, set
the hook and knew straight away it was a good one. Solid cod, right where it should have
been holding in that murky, structure-heavy water. Stayed on that exact depth for another
hour after but couldn't back it up - worth the trip for the one fish alone.
