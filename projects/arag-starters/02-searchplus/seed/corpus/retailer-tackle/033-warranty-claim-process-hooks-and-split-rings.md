# Warranty Claim Process: Hooks and Split Rings

Hooks are treated as a wear item across every Drift Line range and are not covered once a
lure has been fished, since hook-point dulling happens through normal contact with fish,
structure and the bottom rather than through a manufacturing fault.

## Split ring failures

A split ring that opens or fails under normal fighting pressure - not from being pried
open to swap trebles - is assessed as a potential manufacturing defect. Send the lure with
the failed ring still attached where possible, along with a description of what it was
doing when it failed (playing a fish, snagged and pulled, etc.).

## What speeds up a claim

Photos of the failure point, your order confirmation, and a short description of use are
enough for most claims to be assessed without the physical lure needing to be sent in
first.
