# Retrieve Guide: Walk-the-Dog Surface Retrieve

Walk-the-dog is the rhythmic side-to-side rod action that makes a topwater lure zig-zag
across the surface, used on stickbaits and, with a different cadence, poppers.

## The rod action

Point the rod tip low at the water and use short, sharp downward flicks of the wrist with
slack taken up between each flick - the lure should dart left, then right, in a
consistent, walking rhythm rather than a straight retrieve. Keep the cadence steady; an
erratic walk is harder to read than a smooth one and draws fewer strikes.

## On a popper

For the **Backwater Popper 65**, the same short-flick cadence is used to make the cupped
face "chug" and throw water rather than walk side to side - two or three pops, then a
pause of a second or two before the next series.

## On a stickbait

The **Backwater Walk-the-Dog Stickbait 100** is purpose-built for the classic side-to-side
walk, worked continuously with only brief pauses when a fish shows interest but hasn't
struck.

## Best applications

Still water at dawn and dusk for **barramundi** and **Australian bass**; keep it going
right to the rod tip, since topwater strikes often come at the very end of the retrieve.
