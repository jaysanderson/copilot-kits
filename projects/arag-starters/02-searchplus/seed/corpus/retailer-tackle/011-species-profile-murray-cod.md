# Species Profile: Murray Cod

Murray cod hold in the deepest structure available - sunken timber, undercut banks and
rock bars in the Murray-Darling system - and are ambush predators that respond best to a
big-bodied, high-disturbance lure worked slowly through their strike zone.

## Deep, murky-water structure

Cod tolerate and often prefer murky water, where a bigger profile and stronger vibration
carry further than they would in gin-clear conditions.

**Go-to lures:**
- **Tidewalker Deep Vibe 3/4oz**, vertical-jigged directly over deep holes and along the
  edges of sunken timber.
- **Tidewalker Deep Diver 120**, trolled or cast-and-wound past snags and rock bars in
  clearer stretches.
- **Northline Spinnerbait Twin Willow 3/8oz** and **Backwater Crayfish Craw 3"** both take
  cod as a bonus catch when worked for bass or golden perch through the same murky, snaggy
  water.

## Water conditions

Murky/low-visibility water and heavy cover/structure define where cod hold; still, deep
pools are the best secondary water to prioritise, worked slowly.
