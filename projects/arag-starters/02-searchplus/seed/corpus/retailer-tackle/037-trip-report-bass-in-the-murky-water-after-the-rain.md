# Trip Report: Bass in the Murky Water After the Rain

*Submitted by a Drift Line customer.*

Three days of rain had the dam looking like coffee by the time I got out Saturday
morning, and honestly I nearly didn't bother launching. Tied on the **Northline
Spinnerbait Twin Willow 3/8oz** on the recommendation of the guy at the counter and
worked it slow-rolled past every bit of standing timber along the northern arm.

Second fish of the morning was the best bass I've caught this year - sitting right on a
submerged log in water I couldn't see 20cm into. The blade thump must have been the only
thing it had to go on. Ended up with six for the session, all off timber, all on a dead
slow retrieve. Would not have tied on a hardbody in that water again after this trip.
