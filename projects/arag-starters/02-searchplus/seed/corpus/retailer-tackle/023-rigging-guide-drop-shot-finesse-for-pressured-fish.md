# Rigging Guide: Drop-Shot Finesse for Pressured Fish

A drop-shot rig suspends the lure above the sinker rather than below it, letting a small
soft plastic hang and shimmy in place with almost no forward movement - the presentation
of choice for **bream** and **whiting** that have already had a hardbody or paddle-tail go
past without committing.

## Rigging it

Tie the hook mid-line using a Palomar knot so it stands out at a right angle from the main
line, then attach a small sinker to the tag end below it. Thread a **Sable Series Curl
Tail Grub 3"** or a **Sable Series Micro Jig 1/16oz** onto the hook. The lure sits well
above the bottom, in the fish's face without needing forward motion to look alive.

## Working it

Cast tight to structure, let the sinker settle, then shake the rod tip gently without
winding - the lure shimmies in place while barely moving position. This is a stationary,
patient presentation; give it far longer per cast than a retrieve-based lure before moving
on.

## Best applications

Clear-water bream around jetty pylons and pontoons, especially late in a session once
fish have seen faster presentations; whiting over clear sand flats.
