# Water Conditions Guide: Heavy Cover and Structure

Timber, weed beds, rock bars and jetty pylons hold fish precisely because they offer
ambush cover and protection - which means the lures that fish this water well are the ones
built to deflect off structure rather than hang up in it.

## What to tie on

- **Northline Spinnerbait Twin Willow 3/8oz** - the safety-pin wire form is built to
  bump off timber and slide clear rather than snag, the standard choice for dense cover.
- **Backwater Frog Hollow-Body 60** - a weedless hollow-body design for casting directly
  into weed mats and over lily pads for bass and mangrove jack.
- **Backwater Crayfish Craw 3"** - worked with a lift-and-drop presentation right through
  timber for Murray cod and golden perch.
- **Northline Bibbed Minnow 50** - cast tight to jetty pylons and pontoons for bream.

## How tight to cast

Closer than feels comfortable. Fish holding tight to structure won't move far to eat, so a
cast that lands a metre off the timber or pylon is often a metre too far - accept the
occasional snag as the cost of fishing the strike zone properly.
