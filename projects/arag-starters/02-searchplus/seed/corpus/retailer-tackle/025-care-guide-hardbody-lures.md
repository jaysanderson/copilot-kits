# Care Guide: Hardbody Lures

Hardbody lures like the **Tidewalker Shadow Minnow 90**, **Tidewalker Deep Diver 120** and
**Northline Bibbed Minnow 50** are a bigger up-front investment than soft plastics, and a
few minutes of care after each trip keeps them fishing correctly for years rather than
seasons.

## After every saltwater session

Rinse thoroughly in fresh water, including the bib, split rings and treble hooks - salt
crystallises in the hook eyes and bib pivot points and will seize them if left. A soft
brush on the hook shanks clears any residue without damaging the paint finish.

## Hook care

Check hook points after any session where the lure contacted structure or the bottom - a
rolled or blunted point is the single biggest avoidable reason for a lost fish. Touch up
with a hook file rather than replacing the whole treble unless the point has actually
snapped.

## Storage

Store in a lure wrap or divided tray rather than loose in a tackle box - trebles tangling
against each other is the most common cause of hook damage between trips, not use itself.
Keep hardbodies out of direct sun in storage; UV exposure fades paint finishes over a
season or two.
