# Water Conditions Guide: Low Light and Tannin-Stained Water

Dawn, dusk, overcast days and tannin-stained water all share the same effect: reduced
light penetration that pulls predatory fish shallow and switches them onto an aggressive
feeding window. Tannin colour - the tea-brown tint from tea-tree and paperbark run-off
common through northern rivers - behaves like permanent low light even in bright sun.

## What to tie on

- **Backwater Popper 65** - the standard low-light barramundi choice, worked over still
  water at first and last light including tannin-stained rivers.
- **Tidewalker Shadow Minnow 90** - the subsurface follow-up when barra won't commit to
  the surface, its suspend-and-rattle profile built for exactly this water.
- **Northline Surface Cicada 40** - for bass and trout feeding on top in the same
  low-light window on clearer water.

## Why it works

In low light or tannin-stained water, predatory fish shift from relying on sight to
relying on vibration, silhouette and sound - which is why a rattling, suspending or
surface-disturbing lure consistently outperforms a purely visual presentation during these
windows, independent of season.
