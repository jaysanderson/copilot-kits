# Care Guide: Soft Plastics

Soft plastics like the **Sable Series Paddle Tail 4"** and **Sable Series Curl Tail Grub
3"** are made from a plasticised material that reacts with other plastics and with heat  - 
storage matters more for these than for any other lure type Drift Line stocks.

## Colour bleed

Never store different colours in the same compartment for extended periods - plasticiser
migration will bleed colour from a darker plastic into a lighter one touching it,
especially in a hot car or tackle box left in the sun. Keep each colour in its original
resealable pack or a separate compartment.

## Melting other gear

Soft plastics can react with and soften the foam linings of some tackle trays and even
some hardbody paint finishes on prolonged contact. Store plastics separately from
hardbodies rather than mixed in the same tray.

## Extending life per plastic

A paddle tail or grub that's been chewed can usually be re-rigged higher up the body
rather than discarded - push the jighead through a fresh section of plastic above the
damage to get another few fish out of it before replacing.
