# Northline Lipless Rattler 70

**House range:** Northline
**Length:** 70mm | **Weight:** 21g | **Dive depth:** sinking, works any depth on the retrieve
**Price:** $16.95

## Why anglers reach for it

The Lipless Rattler trades the spinnerbait's wide blade thump for a tighter-bodied,
higher-frequency vibration and an internal rattle chamber. In murky water it's a faster
search bait than a spinnerbait - cast it further, cover more water on the retrieve, and
let the rattle do the calling.

## Targets

- **Australian bass** - reaction strikes on a steady retrieve through stained water.
- **Golden perch (yellowbelly)** - worked along current edges where the rattle carries
  over the noise of moving water.

## Best water conditions

- **Murky / low-visibility water** - the rattle and tight vibration are built for exactly
  this.
- **Current / moving water** - casts well across current seams and holds its vibration
  even when swept sideways.

## How to work it

A simple **cast-and-wind steady retrieve** is usually all it needs - no need to work the
rod. In sparser cover than a spinnerbait can handle (scattered rather than dense timber),
just wind it through at a pace that keeps the rattle buzzing. It will hang up in thick
timber where a spinnerbait would deflect clean, so match the water: open murky water or
light cover, Rattler; dense snags, reach for the Twin Willow instead.
