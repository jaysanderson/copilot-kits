# Species Profile: Flathead (Dusky Flathead)

Dusky flathead lie flat and near-invisible on sand and mud flats, ambushing prey that
swims within striking range rather than chasing far. In the clear water typical of most
flats country, they rely on camouflage more than cover, which is why a lure that swims
naturally past at a steady pace out-fishes anything erratic.

## Sand-flats soft plastic fishing

**Sable Series Paddle Tail 4"** is the standard flathead soft plastic - its kick keeps
working on a dead-slow retrieve, which matters because flathead strikes usually come as
the bait passes close rather than from a chase.

**Jighead weight** is the detail that makes or breaks a flats session:
- **1/4oz Sable Series Jighead Round** for typical flats depth, 1-3m, light to moderate
  current - the standard pairing.
- **3/8oz** in deeper water or stronger tidal current so the plastic still gets down and
  stays down.
- **1/8oz** in skinny water under a metre so the retrieve stays slow without dragging
  bottom too hard.

## Water conditions

Clear water and current/moving tidal water are flathead's two strongest conditions  - 
fish the last of the run-out and the first of the run-in over sand edges and drop-offs.
