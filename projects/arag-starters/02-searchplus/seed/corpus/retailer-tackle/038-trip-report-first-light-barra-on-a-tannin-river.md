# Trip Report: First Light Barra on a Tannin River

*Submitted by a Drift Line customer.*

Anchored up before first light on one of the tea-coloured rivers out past the ranger
station - water so dark with tannin you couldn't see the prop wash. Started on the
**Backwater Popper 65**, pop-pop-pause right against the bank, and had a follow within the
first ten casts that didn't eat.

Switched to the **Tidewalker Shadow Minnow 90** and twitched it through the same stretch
with long pauses - four seconds, maybe more. That's when it happened. Barra absolutely
smashed it on the pause, right where the popper had drawn the follow ten minutes earlier.
Landed two more the same way before the sun got too high and the bite shut off. The
popper found them, the suspending minnow closed the deal.
