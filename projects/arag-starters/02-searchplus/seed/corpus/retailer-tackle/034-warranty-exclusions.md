# Warranty Exclusions

The following are not covered under any Drift Line Tackle Co. warranty, across every
range:

- Damage from being snagged on structure and pulled free with force.
- Any lure that has had its hooks, split rings or hardware swapped from factory fitting.
- Normal hook-point wear from fishing.
- Colour fade from extended UV or heat exposure in storage.
- Soft plastic degradation from being stored mixed with other colours or plastics (see the
  *Care Guide: Soft Plastics*).
- Damage from use outside the lure's intended application, such as trolling a
  finesse-rated hardbody at speeds beyond its design.
