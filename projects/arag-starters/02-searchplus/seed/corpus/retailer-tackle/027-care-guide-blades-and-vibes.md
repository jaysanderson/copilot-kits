# Care Guide: Blades and Vibes

Blade and vibration lures like the **Tidewalker Vibe Blade 1/2oz** and **Tidewalker Deep
Vibe 3/4oz** carry treble hooks on split rings at both ends, which means twice the rust
risk of a single-hook lure if they're not rinsed and dried properly.

## Rust prevention

Rinse in fresh water after every session, paying particular attention to the split ring
gap where salt and grit collect and are easy to miss with a quick dip. Dry fully before
returning to a sealed tray - trapped moisture in a closed compartment causes rust faster
than leaving a lure out in the open air.

## Split ring check

Check split rings for the first sign of rust or a ring that's started to open under load  - 
a failed split ring loses the fish and the lure together. Replace at the first sign of
wear rather than waiting for a break.

## Storage

Store blades flat rather than stacked, since the wire form can bend under the weight of
other tackle piled on top, which throws off the vibration action that's the entire point
of the lure.
