# Retrieve Guide: Vertical Jigging Deep Structure

Vertical jigging drops a lure straight down to the fish rather than casting and retrieving
across them - the right approach for **Murray cod** and **golden perch** holding tight to
deep timber and rock bars, where a horizontal retrieve would pass over their heads.

## The technique

Position directly above the structure, drop the **Tidewalker Deep Vibe 3/4oz** to the
bottom, then lift the rod tip sharply and let it fall back on a controlled line - most
strikes come as it falls, so watch the line for any twitch or slack rather than waiting to
feel the bite.

## Depth control

Count the lure down on the drop so you can return it to the same depth repeatedly once you
find where fish are holding - cod in particular often sit at one specific depth along a
timber edge rather than spread through the whole water column.

## Best applications

Deep, murky holes and sunken timber for Murray cod; current edges near drop-offs for
golden perch, worked more slowly than for cod.
