## Why anglers reach for it

The Twin Willow 3/8oz is the spinnerbait we point people to first when the water has
turned murky or is still stirred up from rain. Two willow blades throw a tight column of
flash and vibration that Australian bass can track through low-visibility water using
their lateral line, not their eyes - where a hardbody or soft plastic that relies on sight
loses a lot of its drawing power.

The upturned safety-pin wire also lets it deflect off the timber and rock bars bass hold
against, so it can be worked through cover most lures would hang up in.

## Targets

- **Australian bass** - the standout application: murky dam and river water, slow-rolled
  past standing timber and rock bars.
- **Murray cod** - a larger profile pull with the same blade in stained water over deeper
  structure.

## Best water conditions

- **Murky / low-visibility water** - the single best reason to tie one on. Blade flash and
  thump replace the visual trigger a bass can't get in dirty water.
- **Heavy cover and structure** - the wire form deflects off timber, weed edges and rock
  bars rather than snagging.

## How to work it

Use a **slow-roll retrieve**: cast past the structure, let the spinnerbait sink for a
two-count so the blades are already turning as it clears the cover, then wind at a slow,
steady pace that just keeps the blades ticking over. Don't rip it - the drawing power is
in the blades finding their rhythm, not in fast movement. Bump it off any timber you feel
and pause half a second; that deflection is where most murky-water bass hit.
