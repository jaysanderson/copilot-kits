# ARAG starters - the series

Ten small, finished products on Progress Agentic RAG, one LinkedIn post and article each. Each opens with a problem a business reader recognises, shows the result in five seconds, and ends with three extensions left unbuilt on purpose.

| Day | Product | The problem a business reader recognises | Who they tag | Built from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AnswerGap | Ask ChatGPT about our category and it recommends our competitor. We don't know why, or what to publish to change it. | Head of marketing, founder | chassis + reference |
| 2 | SearchPlus | Our site search returns the wrong product first and we can't change it without a dev ticket. Every bad result is a lost sale. | Ecommerce manager, digital lead | chassis + reference |
| 3 | MediaChat | Customers ask questions our videos and manuals already answer, in languages our team doesn't speak. They get a text wall or nothing. | Customer experience lead, support manager | chassis + reference |
| 4 | EvidenceReader | The AI summary looked fine until someone asked 'where does it say that?' and nobody could show them. | Compliance, legal, anyone who signs off | chassis + reference |
| 5 | TriageDesk | Two hundred applications a week, each read by a person, each decided slightly differently, nobody can explain why. | Operations manager, service delivery lead | finished app, trimmed |
| 6 | OpsAgent | Chat tells us what to do; a person still logs in to five systems to do it. The AI saved nobody any time. | COO, head of operations | chassis + reference |
| 7 | GraphBuilder | The same customer, product or supplier is named six different ways across our documents. Nobody can see how anything connects. | Data lead, knowledge manager | finished app, trimmed |
| 8 | CallSense | Hundreds of recorded calls a month and the only person who knows what customers are saying is the one who was on the call. | Sales leader, customer success lead | finished app, trimmed |

| 10 | ValueCase | We built a business case for AI from guesses. The prospect's own documents held the real numbers and nobody looked. | CFO, sales leader, value engineer | finished app, trimmed |

## The ARAG test (every product, every article)
Swap ARAG for a generic LLM plus a vector store and the product must break or get visibly worse. Each BRIEF names the load-bearing capability. If a build drifts to where ARAG is decoration, stop and fix the design, not the copy.

## Post shape
Quote line, 45-second clip, the pattern in four plain lines, "tag the person who has this problem", three extensions. Article linked in the first comment.

## Publish order versus build order
Publish 1 to 10. Build in `BUILD-ORDER.md` order (finished apps first, AnswerGap last). Batch-record all ten clips before day one.
